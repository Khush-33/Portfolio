## Product Principles

1. **Signal depth immediately**: The first viewport must communicate "this person builds real systems" before any text is read. Visual ambition and technical sophistication should be inseparable.

2. **Real over fabricated**: Every metric, achievement, and claim must be verifiable. Absence of something (internships, testimonials) is better than invention. Let the actual work speak.

3. **Memorable positioning**: In a sea of identical black-and-purple developer portfolios with terminal aesthetics, the space/constellation metaphor must be executed with enough craft and originality that recruiters remember "the space portfolio" when discussing candidates.

4. **Recruiter-optimized journey**: Design for the 30-second scan first, the 3-minute deep-dive second. Most important information (technical depth signals, standout projects, contact) must be immediately accessible. Projects should expand for interested viewers without requiring navigation away.

5. **Mobile-ready sharing**: While initial discovery is desktop, team-sharing happens on mobile. The experience must translate to small screens without losing the core narrative or becoming a generic mobile stack.
